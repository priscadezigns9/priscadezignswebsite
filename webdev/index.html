After launch

Maintenance keeps the website useful.

A website is not finished simply because it has been published. Business information changes, software needs updates and customers depend on the site continuing to work.

What maintenance is for

Maintenance may cover the updates, security checks, performance monitoring, supported technical fixes and content changes stated in the selected maintenance plan.

Maintenance does not automatically include new pages, major redesigns, new features, new integrations, large catalogue changes or work outside the written agreement. Anything outside the scope of the chosen package or agreement will cost extra and will be quoted before work begins.
