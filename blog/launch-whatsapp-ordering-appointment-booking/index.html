Two simpler ways for small businesses to organise customer action

Today, Prisca Dezigns is launching two focused digital products for small businesses: the WhatsApp Ordering Page and the Appointment Booking Page.

Small businesses do not always need a large e-commerce platform, a complex scheduling system or a dashboard filled with features they will never use. What they often need is a clearer way for customers to take the next step. That might mean sending a complete order instead of a string of scattered messages, or requesting an appointment with the service, date and preferred time already organised.

These two new products were created around that practical reality. They are affordable, mobile-friendly pages built by Prisca Dezigns for businesses in Trinidad and Tobago and across the Caribbean that want a better customer experience without taking on an oversized system.

The WhatsApp Ordering Page

Browse. Choose. Send.

The WhatsApp Ordering Page gives customers a branded catalogue where they can browse products or services, choose quantities, add their details and send one organised order to the business on WhatsApp.

It is designed for businesses with a focused catalogue of up to 10 products or services. It does not pretend to be a full online store: there are no customer accounts, inventory dashboard, order history or online payment system in the basic version. Instead, it keeps the familiar WhatsApp conversation while improving the information that reaches the business.

The Appointment Booking Page

Choose a service. Request a time.

The Appointment Booking Page helps customers select a service, choose a preferred date and time, enter their contact details and submit a clear appointment request.

The first version is intentionally simple. The business reviews the request manually and confirms availability directly with the customer. This makes it suitable for salons, barbers, beauty professionals, consultants, coaches, tailors, tutors, photographers, mechanics and other small service providers.

Done-for-you, not do-it-yourself

Prisca Dezigns keeps a private reusable master template for each product. When a client chooses a product, a separate copy is customised with the business logo, colours, information, products or services, policies and destination details. The client does not need to edit code, manage a shared dashboard or build the system alone.

Before production begins, the business provides the required information and pays the agreed deposit. The page is reviewed, tested and approved before publication. Ongoing maintenance is defined work—not a vague fee—and any work outside the selected package or written agreement is quoted and approved before it begins.

Built for the next practical step

The launch of these two products reflects a simple belief: digital tools should make a small business easier to understand and easier to contact. A focused system can be more useful than a complicated one when it solves the exact customer problem in front of the business.

For a business that receives orders through WhatsApp, the WhatsApp Ordering Page brings structure to the conversation. For a business that books time, the Appointment Booking Page brings structure to the request. Both help the customer arrive with better information and help the business spend less time repeating the same questions.
