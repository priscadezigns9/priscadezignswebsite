Who does Prisca Dezigns build websites for?

We build conversion-focused websites and booking systems for Caribbean hotels, guesthouses, restaurants, bars, salons, spas and wellness businesses.

Can you automate WhatsApp for a hospitality business?

Yes. We can support FAQs, menus, service information, enquiries, reservations, appointments, ordering and handoff to staff.

How do you help a business close more bookings?

We connect the website, offer presentation, booking journey and customer follow-up so visitors can understand the offer, choose an option and take action without unnecessary waiting.

Do you serve businesses outside Trinidad and Tobago?

Yes. Prisca Dezigns is based in Trinidad and Tobago and serves hospitality and wellness businesses across the wider Caribbean.
