Service Terms

These terms govern professional services supplied by Prisca Dezigns, including website design, landing pages, chatbots, automations, maintenance and related digital work.

Effective date: August 31, 2026

Important: These terms are a business framework and should be reviewed by a qualified attorney in Trinidad and Tobago before being used as a final customer contract. The signed proposal, quotation or agreement for a project controls the exact price, scope, timeline, package and deliverables.

1. Shared terms for every service

The following rules apply to both website services and AI services.

Agreement and commencement

Work begins only after the client accepts the written proposal or agreement and pays the required deposit. A project slot is not reserved until payment is received.

Deposit and payment

The standard deposit is 50% of the project price, unless the written quotation states another amount. The deposit covers project reservation, planning, setup, scheduling and initial production. Once work begins, the deposit is non refundable except where required by applicable law or the signed agreement.

The remaining balance is due before final delivery, transfer, activation, publication or handover. Prisca Dezigns may pause work and withhold unpaid deliverables when an invoice is overdue.

Client responsibilities

The client must provide accurate business information, content, images, logos, access, feedback and approvals on time. The client must check spelling, prices, contact details, links, claims, artwork and final chatbot responses before approval.

Scope changes

Work not listed in the written scope is not automatically included. New pages, features, integrations, languages, automations, content directions or major changes may require a new quotation and timeline.

Review rounds

The number of included review rounds is determined by the selected package and must appear in the quotation or agreement. One review round means one organised set of feedback submitted together. Additional reviews are billed separately. Changing an approved concept or restarting a project is additional work.

Client delays and cancellation

Late content, feedback, access or payment may extend the timeline. If the client becomes inactive, Prisca Dezigns may pause, reschedule or apply a restart fee.

If the client cancels after work begins, the deposit remains non refundable except where required by law, completed work becomes payable, approved third party costs remain payable, and unpaid work does not have to be delivered.

Final payment and acceptance

Final files, administrator access, transfer of ownership, activation, publication and handover are conditional on full payment. The project is complete after the agreed scope, review process, written approval, final payment and handover are complete.

Ownership

After full payment, the client receives the rights stated in the agreement for the final customised deliverable. Prisca Dezigns retains its pre existing templates, reusable code, design systems, components, automation methods, internal tools, processes and technical know how.

Third party services

Domain, hosting, email, messaging, payment, booking, CRM, database, analytics and automation services are governed by their own terms. Third party fees are separate unless expressly included. Prisca Dezigns is not responsible for outages, price changes, account suspension or policy changes caused by a third party.

Security and credentials

Clients must not send passwords, private API keys or payment card information through ordinary forms, email or chat. Approved delegated access or secure connection methods should be used. Project access may be removed after handover or when the project ends.

Support and maintenance

Support included in the selected package is limited to the period, issues and services stated in the written agreement.

Where a maintenance or support plan is selected, maintenance may cover the services specifically listed in that plan, such as software and dependency updates, security checks, backups, uptime and basic functionality monitoring, supported integration checks, minor content or product updates within the agreed limits, and technical assistance for supported components.

Maintenance does not include new pages, major redesigns, new features, new integrations, payment systems, e-commerce functionality, large catalogue or content updates, copywriting, photography, graphic design, unauthorised changes, third-party subscription charges, domain registration or renewal, hosting charges unless expressly included, or work caused by third-party services outside Prisca Dezigns’ control.

A correction to an implementation error may qualify as support where the issue relates to the agreed deliverable. Anything outside the scope of the chosen package, maintenance plan or written agreement will be treated as additional work and will cost extra. Additional work will be quoted or priced before it begins and will require client approval.

Maintenance is not automatically required after launch. The client may choose self-management, pay-as-needed support or an ongoing maintenance plan, subject to the applicable agreement. Any recurring fee will be explained by the specific services included.

No guaranteed results

Prisca Dezigns does not guarantee a specific number of leads, sales, bookings, followers, rankings, advertising results, revenue or funding outcome.

Confidentiality and portfolio

Confidential information is handled according to the agreement and Privacy Policy. Unless the client requests confidentiality in writing, Prisca Dezigns may display completed work for portfolio, educational or case study purposes.

Disputes and liability

The parties should first attempt good faith written resolution. To the extent permitted by law, Prisca Dezigns is not responsible for indirect losses, lost revenue, lost opportunities or third party failures outside the agreed scope. The final agreement should contain liability wording reviewed by a local attorney.

WEBSITE SERVICES ONLY

2. Website Services Policy

These terms apply specifically to websites, landing pages, online stores, template customisation and related web development.

Included work

The written agreement must state the number of pages, sections, features, forms, integrations, copywriting, image support, SEO, hosting, domain setup, analytics, training and reviews included.

Content and approval

The client supplies or approves business content, prices, images, policies and claims. Approved content may not be changed without additional work if the change alters the agreed direction.

Launch and handover

Publication, domain connection, source files and administrator access occur according to the agreement and after final payment. A client is responsible for maintaining accounts and renewing third party services after handover unless a maintenance plan says otherwise.

Website forms

Forms are configured according to the agreed destination, such as email, database, CRM, booking or messaging service. The client must approve the fields, notifications and destination before launch.

AI SERVICES ONLY

3. AI Services Policy

These terms apply specifically to chatbots, AI voice agents, AI customer service, WhatsApp or email automation, lead qualification, reporting and related AI systems.

Configuration and knowledge

The client must provide accurate approved business information, services, prices, FAQs, policies, tone, restricted topics, escalation rules and knowledge sources. The system is configured from the information and instructions approved by the client.

Human review and handoff

AI systems are tools that operate within configured instructions and connected services. They may require monitoring and updates. The agreement should identify when a conversation is handed to a human and who receives the lead or alert.

Accuracy and limitations

AI responses may require human review and are not guaranteed to be correct in every situation. The client must not rely on an AI system as a substitute for professional legal, medical, financial, emergency or regulatory advice unless a separate written arrangement specifically covers that use.

Channels and integrations

The agreement must identify approved channels and integrations, such as website chat, WhatsApp, email, calendar, CRM, database, payment or booking tools. Access and third party charges are handled under the shared terms.

AI safety

Prisca Dezigns may refuse or suspend an AI workflow that is unlawful, deceptive, abusive, unsafe, privacy invasive or outside the agreed purpose. Sensitive information should not be submitted unless the service has been specifically assessed and approved.

AI review rounds and updates

AI review rounds include organised changes to approved prompts, flows, knowledge or responses within the selected package. New channels, new automations, major knowledge changes or new business purposes may require additional fees.
